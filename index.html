Selection logic

How We Selected S07 & S03

We screened all 10 stores before selecting the two stores for deeper operational investigation.

Decision

Why S07 & S03?

We screened all 10 stores using P&L, contribution per order and order volume. S07 had the largest P&L loss and negative contribution per order. S03 had the second-largest P&L loss and very weak contribution per order. Both handle roughly 132–135 orders per day, so low demand alone does not explain their weak economics. S09 shows a different low-volume pattern and was retained as a comparison case.

Comparison case

Why not S09?

S09 is also loss-making, but its much lower order volume and healthier contribution per order suggest a different scale/rent issue. It was kept as a comparison case rather than the primary operational deep-dive.

Screening used only the three measures shown — no composite score or weights. Selection identifies where to investigate; it does not by itself establish causes.

Validation / discrepancy note
The earlier “≈83% of S03/S07 deliveries are over 10 km” figure was excluded and is not used anywhere in this dashboard. Recomputed from raw Trip_Logs, the longest valid trip is 5.4 km and 0% of trips exceed 10 km; average distance is ≈3.0 km at every store. Two further notes: the Trip_Logs Delivery_Performance flag marks ≥10 min as late (S03 77%, S07 76%), while this dashboard uses the SQL definition of more than 10 min (72%). And promised_minutes in orders is 15 for every order; against 15 minutes, late rates are 34% at S03/S07 vs about 10% elsewhere — the gap holds under any threshold.

Methodology & Definitions

Delivered contribution = Net Amount − COGS − Delivery Cost

Returned contribution = −(COGS + Delivery Cost)

Cancelled contribution = 0

Store P&L = Contribution − Monthly Rent × 1.4 months

Late delivery = Pickup-to-delivery time > 10 minutes

Analysis period = 42 days (1.4 months of rent)

Benchmarks: “other 8” = the eight stores other than S03 and S07, simple average of store-level values. Delivery cost benchmark is ₹28/order (other-8 average ≈ ₹27.99). Monthly opportunity = 42-day gap × 30/42.

COO Decision

Priority Investigation: S07 & S03

Both stores handle substantial volume and show materially weaker per-order economics. The drivers below are associated with that gap; they are not proven causes.

These opportunities are separate and should not be added directly because effects may overlap. A returned order with FRESH50 and a long delivery sits in all three, and none is a guaranteed saving.
